# The Entry List holds still

An Entry List narrowed by Unread Only used to drop an Entry as soon as it stopped being unread. The Entry being read was spared until the reader moved off it (see 0009 and 0010), but moving off it removed it, so reading down a narrowed list meant rows disappearing from under the cursor at every step — the reader's own progress rearranging the thing they were reading. The Entry List is now whatever the last query returned, and no state change takes an Entry out of it. Read, Starred and Archived are shown where the Entry already sits: the dot clears, the title dims, the icons appear.

The list is rebuilt only when the reader asks for a different list — choosing a Collection, including the one already showing, changing the sort, refreshing the Feeds — which is also the whole of the rule, stated in one sentence a reader can hold. Mark-all-read consequently leaves every row in place and Read, and keeps the cursor and the pagination cursor it would otherwise have had to discard.

The alternative was exempting only the automatic Read that arriving in the Reading Pane sets, and keeping the removal for the reader's own triage. That keeps a narrowed list honest at every instant, at the price of a per-Collection exemption table nobody can predict from the outside: an Entry unstarred inside Starred vanishes, an Entry read inside Unread Only does not. It also kept the membership predicate that five separate sites had to consult — the optimistic path, both rollback paths, mark-all-read, and a `leave()` hook wired into every selection change — all of which this decision deletes.

The cost is a narrowed list that is briefly out of date: it shows Entries that are no longer unread, and a reader who wants them gone chooses Unread again or refreshes. Two consequences follow and are intended. Unstarring an Entry inside Starred leaves the row visible until the list is rebuilt. Archiving the selected Entry no longer advances to the next one, because there is no gap to fall into; the Archived icon on the row it stayed on is the confirmation, and `j` moves on.
